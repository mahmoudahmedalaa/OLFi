UAE KYC framework 















	•	Customer Identification Program (CIP): Verifying the customer's identity using official documents. (Stage 1) 
	•	Customer Due Diligence (CDD): Gathering information to understand the customer's activities and assess risk. (Stage 1) 
	•	Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): Applying additional scrutiny for higher-risk customers or transactions. (Stage 2)

AML Policy Structure (You Need This Before Launch)
Even if you’re not a bank.
Your AML framework must include:
A. Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
	•	Identity verification procedures
	•	PEP screening rules
	•	Enhanced due diligence triggers
B. Risk-Based Approach
Define:
	•	Low risk: salaried employee, stable income
	•	Medium risk: multiple high-value loans
	•	High risk: foreign PEP, cash-heavy background
C. Suspicious Activity Reporting
If user:
	•	Submits fake ID
	•	Uses mismatched identity
	•	Appears on sanctions list
You must:
	•	Flag internally
	•	Possibly report (depending on severity)
D. Record Keeping
Keep:
	•	ID documents
	•	Verification logs
	•	Consent logs
	•	Screening results
Retention: Usually 5 years
E. MLRO Appointment
You must assign: Money Laundering Reporting Officer
Even in small team.

UAE Regulatory License Path
Your classification depends on what you do.
If we:
	•	Aggregate debts
	•	Show refinance offers
	•	Refer users to banks
	•	Do NOT hold funds
	•	Do NOT make lending decisions
You likely fall under:
Option A – Commercial Broker / Financial Services Brokerage (Non-Lender)
Possible structures:
	•	Mainland LLC with activity: “Credit Brokerage”
	•	DIFC fintech license (if positioning as tech infra)
	•	ADGM fintech innovation license
Most practical for early stage: Mainland commercial brokerage license + proper AML framework
We do NOT initially need:
	•	Full Central Bank lender license
	•	Deposit-taking approval
	•	Payment service provider license (if no fund handling)
If we are Later:
	•	Handle repayments
	•	Take instalment % directly from banks
	•	Intermediate funds
Then you may trigger:
	•	CBUAE supervision
	•	Payment services regulation
	•	Stored value facility rules
Do You Automatically Need an Open Finance License?
Not necessarily.
It depends on how you access the data.
There are 2 models:
Model A — You Integrate Through a Licensed Open Finance Provider (Recommended Early)
Example providers in UAE:
	•	Tarabut
	•	Lean Technologies
In this model:
	•	They hold the regulatory relationship
	•	They are licensed / approved TPPs
	•	They handle secure data pipes
	•	You act as a client of theirs
Result:
You DO NOT need your own Open Finance license (initially).
This is the safest and smartest Phase 1 route.
Model B — You Connect Directly to Banks via Open Finance APIs
If you:
	•	Register as Third Party Provider (TPP)
	•	Connect directly to banks
	•	Store raw bank data
Then you likely need:
	•	CBUAE authorization as Open Finance participant
	•	Strong cybersecurity certification
	•	Regulatory reporting
	•	Possibly capital requirements
This is heavy.
Not recommended at MVP stage.
What License Do You Need for BuyOut Phase 1?
If your structure is:
	•	Marketplace
	•	Referral platform
	•	Debt dashboard
	•	No fund custody
	•	Using Lean/Tarabut for data access
Then:
You likely need:
✔ Commercial license (Financial Brokerage / Credit Brokerage activity) ✔ AML policy ✔ Data protection compliance ✔ Contract with licensed Open Finance provider
You do NOT need:
✖ Open Finance TPP license ✖ Payment service provider license ✖ Lending license
As long as you: Do not hold funds.
Open Finance Compliance You Still Must Follow
Even if Lean/Tarabut is licensed:
You must:
	•	Obtain explicit user consent
	•	Clearly disclose data usage
	•	Store data securely
	•	Allow user data deletion
	•	Not reuse data beyond consent scope
Consent must be:
Clear. Granular. Time-bound.
What Triggers Regulatory Upgrade?
You trigger deeper regulation if you:
	•	Store full transaction histories long-term
	•	Monetize raw financial data
	•	Repackage financial data to third parties
	•	Provide account information services independently
	•	Initiate payments
Then CBUAE oversight increases.
Strategic Recommendation for BuyOut
Phase 1 (MVP):
✔ Use Lean or Tarabut ✔ Stay broker / marketplace ✔ Do not hold funds ✔ Do not initiate payments ✔ Store minimal necessary financial data
Phase 2:
✔ Explore direct Open Finance registration ✔ Consider embedded payment license ✔ Expand compliance team
Why This Is Important for Investors
If you say: “We pull bank data.”
Investors will ask:
Under which regulatory structure?
You must confidently answer:
“We integrate via licensed Open Finance providers under UAE framework.”
That signals maturity.
The Big Risk If Done Wrong
If you bypass Open Finance rules:
	•	Banks block integration
	•	CBUAE can fine or suspend
	•	Insurance won’t cover data breach
	•	Expansion to KSA becomes impossible
Fintech regulation compounds.
Final Answer
At this stage:
You do NOT need your own Open Finance license.
You DO need:
✔ Commercial brokerage license ✔ AML/KYC framework ✔ Contract with licensed Open Finance provider ✔ Proper consent management ✔ Strong cybersecurity posture

Missing from framework and Open finance path:
	•	Formal legal license mapping opinion
	•	Defined EDD triggers
	•	Ongoing monitoring logic
	•	Data protection documentation
	•	Vendor governance framework
