FAQ

Questions answered.

Is OLFI free for borrowers?
Yes, completely free. OLFI earns a success fee from partner lenders when a refinance is completed — you pay nothing. Browse offers, check your OLFI Score, and compare deals at zero cost.
Will checking my OLFI Score affect my credit?
No. OLFI performs a soft data pull for your score and offer matching — this does not create a hard inquiry on your AECB credit file. Only when you formally apply to a specific lender does a hard check occur.
What loans can I refinance through OLFI?
OLFI covers personal loans, credit card debt, and auto loans for salaried residents in the UAE. BNPL consolidation and business loans are planned for later phases.
Is my financial data safe?
OLFI uses regulated open-banking infrastructure. Your data is read-only — OLFI cannot move money. All data is encrypted and handled in compliance with UAE data-protection regulations. Your data is never sold.
Are the Sharia-compliant products genuinely certified?
Yes. OLFI's Sharia supervisory board certifies all product structures (Murābaḥa, Tawarruq, Ijāra) and conducts quarterly audits. Profit rates and payment structures are fully transparent — no hidden conventional interest.
When does OLFI launch?
We're targeting a UAE sandbox launch in Q4 2026 via the CBUAE fintech sandbox. Early access users on the waitlist get priority onboarding, lifetime freemium access, and an AED 100 cashback credit on their first referral.
