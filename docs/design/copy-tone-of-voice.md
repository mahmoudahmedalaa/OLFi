# BuyOut Copy & Tone of Voice Guidelines

Because BuyOut deals with personal debt, loans, and credit scores, our tone of voice is our most critical UX tool. We must build trust immediately. Application copy must never be confusing, overwhelming, or alarmist.

## The 3 Pillars of BuyOut's Tone

1.  **Empowering, Not Judgmental**
    *   *Bad:* "You are spending too much on your credit card."
    *   *Good:* "Let's explore options to lower your monthly payments."
2.  **Concise & Premium**
    *   *Bad:* "Please tap here to submit your application to the bank for review."
    *   *Good:* "Apply Now."
3.  **Transparent & Safe**
    *   *Bad:* "We'll connect to your bank."
    *   *Good:* "Connect securely using UAE Pass. We never store your credentials."

## Islamic Finance Terminology (Strictly Enforced)

As a Sharia-compliant platform, we must use precise terminology to maintain trust and legal transparency. Do not use conventional banking terms.

*   **NEVER Use:** "Interest", "Interest Rate", "Loan", "Borrowing".
*   **ALWAYS Use:** "Expected Profit Rate" (or "Profit Rate"), "Financing Cost", "Financing Facility", "Islamic Finance".
*   **Refinancing Context:** When communicating paying off conventional debt, use terms like "Consolidating to a Sharia-compliant facility", "Ethical Balance Transfer", or "Transitioning your debt." Avoid "Refinancing your loan."

## Standardized Phrasing Dictionary

To ensure consistency across the application, always use the agreed-upon phrasing for common actions:

| Action / State | Approved Phrasing |
| :--- | :--- |
| Initializing App | "Optimizing your financial profile..." |
| Calculating Results | "Analyzing top consolidation offers..." |
| Loading Bank Selection | "Fetching eligible UAE bank products..." |
| Error (Network) | "Connection lost. Please try again." |
| Error (Ineligible) | "Based on Central Bank guidelines, we couldn't find a matching offer today." |
| Success (App Submitted) | "Application Submitted. A partner representative will contact you shortly." |

## Capitalization Rules
*   **Sentence Case:** Use sentence case for paragraphs, descriptions, and subtitles. (e.g., "Enter your monthly salary.")
*   **Title Case (Start Case):** Use Title Case for Buttons, Header Titles, and Bank Product Names. (e.g., "Apply Now", "Personal Loan Details").
*   **AED Prefix:** Always put the currency code *before* the number, separated by a space. (e.g., `AED 15,000` not `15000 AED` or `15,000AED`).
