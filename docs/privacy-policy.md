# Privacy Policy for BuyOut

**Effective Date: February 22, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to BuyOut ("Company", "we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect
We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:
- **Personal Data:** Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address (e.g., when you register or sign in), demographic information, and encrypted identification data (like UAE Pass details, when applicable).
- **Financial Data:** Information related to your current loans, debts, income (salary), and EMI payments that you manually input into our calculators and debt consolidation engines.
- **Usage Data:** Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Service, such as your device ID, operating system, and interactions within the app.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Create, manage, and secure your account.
- Calculate potential refinance savings, EMI, and debt-to-income (DTI) ratios.
- Match you with potential bank products and offers.
- Improve our mathematical algorithms and consolidation engines.
- Respond to customer service requests and send administrative information.

## 4. Disclosure of Your Information
We do not sell your personal data. We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations:
- **By Law or to Protect Rights:** If we believe the release of information about you is necessary to respond to legal process, to investigate or remedy potential violations of our policies, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of others.
- **Third-Party Service Providers:** We may share your information with third parties that perform services for us or on our behalf, such as data analysis, email delivery, hosting services (e.g., Supabase), and customer service.
- **Financial Partners:** With your explicit consent (e.g., when you apply for an offer), we may share necessary loan and demographic details with partnered financial institutions to process your application.

## 5. Security of Your Information
We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.
