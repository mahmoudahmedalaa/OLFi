# Privacy Policy for OLFi

**Last Updated and Effective Date: February 22, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to OLFi ("Company", "we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the applicable data protection laws of the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and other relevant regulations. This Privacy Policy comprehensively details how we collect, use, process, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, website, and related financial services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using the Service, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you must immediately discontinue use of the Service.

## 2. Definitions
*   **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including but not limited to name, email address, identification numbers (e.g., Emirates ID, UAE Pass), and geographic location.
*   **Financial Data:** Specific personal information pertaining to your economic situation, including salary, outstanding debts, loan tenures, Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) amounts, and credit scores (e.g., Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB) data).
*   **Processing:** Any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, such as collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, or retrieval.

## 3. Information We Collect
We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with you. The categories of information we collect include:

### A. Information You Provide Directly
*   **Account Registration Data:** Name, email address, phone number, secure passwords, or biometric authentication tokens.
*   **Financial Information:** Details regarding your current loans, credit cards, auto loans, mortgages, your monthly salary, employer name, and any other data you manually input into our calculators and debt consolidation engines.
*   **Verification Data:** Government-issued identification (e.g., Emirates ID copy), salary certificates, and bank statements uploaded during the offer application process.
*   **Communication Data:** Information you provide when contacting our customer support, participating in surveys, or providing feedback.

### B. Information Automatically Collected
*   **Device and Usage Data:** Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Service, including your IP address, device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, browser type, and interactions within the app (e.g., pages viewed, time spent, buttons clicked).
*   **Location Data:** Approximate location data derived from your IP address or precise geolocation (if you grant explicit permission) to provide region-specific offers and comply with local regulations.

### C. Information From Third Parties
*   **Credit Bureaus and Financial Institutions:** With your explicit and verifiable consent, we may receive data regarding your credit history, existing liabilities, and credit score from authorized entities such as AECB to provide accurate refinancing offers.
*   **Integration Partners:** Data retrieved via integrations like UAE Pass for seamless KYC and authentications.

## 4. How We Use Your Information
We strictly limit the Processing of your data to specific, legitimate purposes:
1.  **Service Delivery:** To create, manage, and secure your account, authenticate your identity, and provide the core functionalities of the OLFi app.
2.  **Financial Calculations & Matching:** To accurately calculate potential refinance savings, EMI restructuring, and Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratios. We use financial algorithms to match your profile with the most suitable banking products and consolidation offers available in the market.
3.  **Application Processing:** To facilitate and securely transmit your loan application and supporting documents to our partnered financial institutions when you explicitly choose to 'Accept Offer'.
4.  **Service Improvement:** To analyze usage trends, debug errors, and improve our mathematical algorithms and consolidation engines.
5.  **Communication:** To send you technical notices, security alerts, support messages, and administrative information regarding changes to our terms or policies.
6.  **Compliance and Protection:** To comply with applicable legal obligations in the UAE (e.g., Anti-Money Laundering regulations), enforce our Terms of Use, investigate fraudulent activities, and protect the rights, property, and safety of the Company, our users, and others.

## 5. Disclosure and Sharing of Your Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Data. We only disclose your information in the following circumstances:

*   **Financial Partners & Banks:** When you explicitly elect to proceed with a refinancing offer, we securely transmit the necessary Personal and Financial Data to the respective banking institution to underwrite and process your application.
*   **Third-Party Service Providers:** We may share data with trusted vendors who perform services on our behalf, including secure cloud hosting (e.g., Supabase/AWS), data analytics, email delivery, SMS gateways, and secure document verification services. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
*   **Legal & Regulatory Authorities:** If mandated by law, court order, or governmental regulation, we may disclose your information to law enforcement, regulatory bodies, or other third parties to comply with legal processes or protect vital interests.
*   **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the business assets, subject to the recipient adhering to this Privacy Policy.

## 6. Data Security, Storage, and Retention
We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data:
*   **Encryption:** All data in transit is secured using TLS/SSL protocols. Sensitive Financial Data stored in our database (e.g., Supabase PostgreSQL) is protected by strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring it is only accessible to authorized users.
*   **Anonymization:** Where feasible, data used for analytics is aggregated and anonymized.
*   **Retention:** We retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (e.g., for tax, accounting, or regulatory compliance). Upon account deletion, your data will be securely erased or anonymized within 30 days.

*Disclaimer:* While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights and Choices
Depending on your jurisdiction, and in alignment with UAE data protection principles, you may have the following rights:
*   **Access:** Request copies of your Personal Data held by us.
*   **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information.
*   **Deletion (Right to be Forgotten):** Request the deletion of your account and associated Personal Data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
*   **Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal, but may prevent us from providing certain services (e.g., fetching new bank offers).
*   **Opt-Out:** Unsubscribe from promotional communications using the provided links in emails or via app settings.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the details provided in Section 10. We may require verification of your identity before processing your request.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 21 (the legal age of majority for financial contracts in the UAE). We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

## 9. Modifications to this Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the revised Policy within the app, updating the "Effective Date," and, where appropriate, sending an in-app notification or email. Continued use of the Service after modifications signifies your acceptance of the updated Policy.
